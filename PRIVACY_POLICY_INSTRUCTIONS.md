# Privacy Policy Publishing Instructions

## 📁 Files Created

1. **PRIVACY_POLICY.md** - Markdown version for documentation
2. **privacy_policy_website.html** - Styled HTML version for websites
3. **privacy_policy_google_sites.txt** - Formatted text for Google Sites

## 🌐 How to Publish to Google Sites

### Method 1: Using the HTML File (Recommended)

1. **Go to your Google Site**: https://sites.google.com/view/jcma-portfolio/home
2. **Click "Edit"** (pencil icon in top right)
3. **Create a new page**:
   - Click "Pages" in the right panel
   - Click "+ New page"
   - Name it: "Privacy Policy - Solomon Prayer Compass"
4. **Add content**:
   - Click "Insert" → "Embed"
   - Choose "Embed code"
   - Open `privacy_policy_website.html` in a text editor
   - Copy the ENTIRE content
   - Paste it into the embed box
   - Click "Insert"
5. **Publish**:
   - Click "Publish" in top right
   - Confirm publication
6. **Get the URL**:
   - Copy the page URL (e.g., https://sites.google.com/view/jcma-portfolio/privacy-policy)

### Method 2: Using Text Format (Simpler)

1. **Go to your Google Site**: https://sites.google.com/view/jcma-portfolio/home
2. **Click "Edit"**
3. **Create a new page**:
   - Click "Pages" → "+ New page"
   - Name it: "Privacy Policy - Solomon Prayer Compass"
4. **Add content**:
   - Click in the content area
   - Open `privacy_policy_google_sites.txt`
   - Copy all content
   - Paste into Google Sites
5. **Format** (optional):
   - Select headings and make them larger/bold
   - Add colors if desired
6. **Publish** and copy the URL

## 📱 Update Google Play Console

After publishing to Google Sites:

1. **Go to Google Play Console**: https://play.google.com/console
2. **Select your app**: "Solomon Prayer Compass"
3. **Navigate to**: Policy → App content
4. **Privacy Policy section**:
   - Click "Start" or "Edit"
   - Paste the URL from Google Sites
   - Example: `https://sites.google.com/view/jcma-portfolio/privacy-policy`
   - Click "Save"
5. **Submit for review**

## 🍎 Update Apple App Store Connect

1. **Go to App Store Connect**: https://appstoreconnect.apple.com
2. **Select your app**
3. **App Information**:
   - Find "Privacy Policy URL"
   - Paste the Google Sites URL
   - Save changes

Make sure this URL is:
