# Privacy Policy for Solomon Prayer Compass App

**Last updated: March 4, 2025**

## Introduction

Thank you for using the Solomon Prayer Compass app! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Location Data
- **Purpose**: Our app collects your precise location data solely to calculate and display the compass direction towards the Temple of Solomon based on your current location.
- **Usage**: This information is processed locally on your device and is NOT stored, transmitted, or shared with any third parties.
- **When Collected**: Location data is accessed only when you actively use the compass feature and only with your explicit permission.
- **User Control**: You can revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings.

### Device Information
- **What We Collect**: Basic device information such as device model, operating system version, and app version.
- **Purpose**: This information is used solely to ensure app compatibility and improve functionality.
- **Storage**: Device information is not linked to your personal identity.

### Video and Media Access
- **Purpose**: Our app may access media files stored on your device to play instructional videos within the app.
- **Important**: We do NOT collect, store, copy, or share any of your media content with third parties.
- **Scope**: Media access is limited to files you explicitly choose to play within the app.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:
1. **Core Functionality**: Providing compass direction calculations to the Temple of Solomon
2. **App Improvement**: Enhancing app features and performance
3. **Technical Support**: Responding to user inquiries and resolving technical issues

## Information Sharing

- **No Sale of Data**: We do NOT sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties.
- **No Third-Party Sharing**: Your location data and personal information are never shared with third parties.
- **Anonymous Analytics**: We may use aggregated, anonymous usage statistics to improve app performance (no personal data included).

## Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:
- **Local Processing**: Location calculations are performed locally on your device
- **No Cloud Storage**: We do not store your personal data on external servers
- **Secure Permissions**: We only request necessary permissions and handle them securely

## Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following system services:
- **Location Services**: Built-in device location services (iOS/Android)
- **Compass Services**: Built-in device compass/magnetometer
- **Video Player**: Local video playback capabilities

**Important**: We do not integrate with external third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

## Your Rights and Choices

- **Permission Control**: You can grant or revoke location permissions through your device settings
- **Data Deletion**: Since we don't store your data externally, uninstalling the app removes all local data
- **Contact Us**: You can contact us with any privacy concerns (see contact information below)

## Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of significant changes by:
- Posting the updated policy in the app
- Updating the "Last updated" date
- Providing notice through app updates when possible

## Compliance

This Privacy Policy complies with:
- Google Play Store requirements
- Apple App Store guidelines
- General data protection principles
